---
title: Schedules and evacuation
description: Warn players, choose safe destinations, and schedule predictable resets.
---
Configure warnings early enough for players to leave voluntarily, then define a destination that is loaded, permanent, and outside the managed world. Test evacuation with staff before enabling unattended schedules. Schedule times should be documented in the server’s player-facing timezone even when stored or logged in UTC.

Changing a schedule after a warning can prevent the warned reset from running; integrations should treat warnings as plans, not guarantees.
