## What happens during a reset?

<Steps>
1. **Check the world.** RWR confirms the provider, world identity, destination, and current reset state.
2. **Warn and evacuate players.** Scheduled warnings give players time to finish, then RWR moves everyone to a safe loaded world.
3. **Regenerate through the world provider.** Multiverse-Core or Worlds performs the authoritative world operation.
4. **Verify before unlocking.** RWR checks that the replacement world is registered, loaded, and safe, then records the result in history.
</Steps>
